About Amityx

Technology with a human touch.

Amityx comes from "amity", meaning friendship. We believe AI should feel approachable, trustworthy and useful to ordinary people, not just large enterprises.

We are a team of engineers, designers and data specialists building AI-powered products and custom solutions for individuals, startups and growing businesses.

🎯 Mission

Make powerful AI simple and accessible to everyone.

🔭 Vision

A world where software understands people.

💎 Values

Transparency, privacy, craftsmanship and partnership.
